Grove privacy policy

Effective 30 September 2026. Grove is made by MTWABP In.

In short: Grove does not collect your data. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It works without the internet. Your habits stay on your phone.

What Grove keeps on your phone

To work, Grove saves these in its own storage on your phone:

  • Your habits: their names, plants, schedules, goals and reminder times.
  • Their history: the days you did them and how much, urges you resisted, slips, and when each change happened.
  • Your achievements, and what you have found in your valley.
  • Your settings, such as sounds, reminders, and your place for the sky.

Grove does not send any of this anywhere. The app does not even have permission to use the internet.

Your place

  • Grove uses a place to show the sun and the moon where they really are, and to pick your seasons. Giving one is optional.
  • You choose a city from a list built into the app, or type coordinates. If you skip it, Grove guesses from your phone's time zone.
  • Grove does not have permission to read your location, and never knows where your phone is.
  • The place you choose is stored and used on your phone only.

Notifications

Reminders are planned on your phone and come from your phone. They only come if you turn them on.

Backups

  • When you choose Backup in Grove, it makes a file with your habits and settings. You decide where the file goes: a folder on your phone, or another app you pick. Keep it somewhere private.
  • If your phone's own backup is turned on, Android can include Grove's habits and settings in your device backup to your Google account. Google handles that backup under its own privacy policy. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Sharing

Grove can make a picture of your progress or your valley. It leaves your phone only if you choose to share it, through your phone's share menu, to an app you pick.

Permissions

  • Notifications (optional): for reminders.
  • Run at startup: to plan reminders and widget updates again after your phone restarts.
  • Keep the screen on (optional): in zen mode, if you ask for it.
  • Storage, only on Android 10 and older: to save pictures to your gallery.

Children

Grove is meant for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete any habit in Grove. To remove everything, uninstall Grove or clear its storage in your phone's settings. Backup files you made are yours to delete.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be on this page with a new date. Grove will never start collecting data without saying so here first.
